Search engines that block are paused instead of being asked again and again.

### Highlights
- **Pause for blocking engines.** An engine that answers with a CAPTCHA, «too many requests» or access denied is no longer queried on every search (which is what kept the block alive): it stays out for 15 minutes, then 30, 60… up to 6 hours, while the others keep searching. When the pause ends it gets a single query back; if it answers it is back to normal, if it blocks again the pause doubles. No CAPTCHA is bypassed and no address is changed: the portal simply waits.
- The touchiest engines (DuckDuckGo, Startpage, Brave, Google, Bing) are queried at most once every 30–60 seconds even when they answer; Google CSE and Yandex keep the normal pace. The direct DuckDuckGo fallback is skipped while it is paused or was just queried.
- When every chosen engine is paused, a code is not searched in vain: it restarts by itself when the pause ends and does not count as a deferral.
- The «Why?» diary shows paused engines in grey with the time they resume; *Settings → How the search went* shows «paused» with the resume time.
- `searxng/settings.yml`: ahmia, torch and wikidata removed; they are useless for codes and filled the log with errors at every start.
