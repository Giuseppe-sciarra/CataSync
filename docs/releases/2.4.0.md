A Warehouse tab to manage the products that are already on the shop, straight from the portal.

### Highlights
- **Warehouse**: categories on the left, their products on the right (subcategories included), search by name, SKU or EAN, pages of 15, 20, 30, 50 or 100.
- **Edit category and price** of a product from the list; the change goes to the site at once.
- **Delete** moves the product to the **site's trash** — WooCommerce's own, the same as *Products → Trash* in WordPress — so it disappears from the shop but can still be recovered.
- **Trash view**: the site's trash, products trashed from WordPress included, with *Restore* (previous status and address), *Delete permanently* and *Empty the trash*. WordPress empties it by itself after `EMPTY_TRASH_DAYS` (30 by default); the portal takes the same number per shop to show the right dates.
- The **Guide** is now three guides — products, warehouse, categories — with screenshots.
