Two-step verification for everyone, and a maintenance notice on the sign-in page.

### Highlights
- **Maintenance notice**: under *Settings → Maintenance notice* you switch it on with a day, a start time, an end time (also across midnight) and an optional note, with a live preview. It shows above email and password on the sign-in page, with day and times in bold in the reader's language; while the work is on it becomes «Maintenance in progress», and once the end time has passed it disappears by itself. The public page does not show it.
- **Two-step verification** for clients and administrators, from the new **Account** page (click your email at the top): **authenticator app** (QR code or a key to type), **email code** (six digits, ten minutes, through the SMTP server in `.env`) and **passkey** (fingerprint, face, device PIN or security key). Any combination can be on; after the password you pick the one at hand.
- Ten one-time **backup codes** come with the first method, to copy or download; they can be regenerated from *Account*.
- Until it is on, a **small line** at the top of the panel and a dot next to your email remind you; once it is on, they go away.
- Under *Users*, a **2FA** column and **Remove 2FA** for people who lost phone and codes. An administrator locked out can run `python -m app.due_fattori togli <email>` inside the container.
- The **password** can now be changed from *Account*.
- Turning a method on, changing or turning it off, removing a passkey or regenerating the codes asks for the password again (valid for ten minutes). Wrong codes are counted in the database (six every ten minutes per user, also under parallel requests) and every code or passkey challenge works exactly once.
- Fixed: the in-memory sign-in limits no longer reset all at once when more than 5000 keys pile up.

### Upgrading
New Python dependencies (`webauthn`, `segno`, plus pinned `pyOpenSSL`, `cbor2`, `asn1crypto`): rebuild the image (`docker compose up -d --build`). The database gains its new columns and the `passkey` table by itself on start. Passkeys need HTTPS and are bound to the domain in `PUBLIC_URL`.
