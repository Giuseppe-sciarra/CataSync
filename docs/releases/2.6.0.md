A public page for the portal, with a «Request information» form sent by email and a privacy policy.

### Highlights
- **Public page** at the portal's address, in a dark, product-style design: a hero with the real panel, the problem (by hand vs. with the portal), five everyday examples that rotate on their own with the real screen, how it works in four steps with a live barcode that checks the final digit, the features with a recording of the panel, monthly and yearly **prices** (saving, free months and a bar comparison), FAQ and a closing panel with the email.
- **Sign in** and **Request information** at the top. The form (plan, first name, last name, email, message; the plan is already selected when you come from that price's button and ends up in the email) is sent through the SMTP server configured in `.env` (`SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `SMTP_SECURITY`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD`, `SMTP_FROM`, `CONTACT_TO`), with Reply-To set to the sender; a hidden trap field and a per-address limit keep spam out.
- A default **privacy policy** written for the portal, or your own; **company details** in the footer. Everything is set from the new *Public page* tab in the admin panel, which can also switch the page off.
- **Specifications and filters** with TD Product Specs: rows a product already has, hand-written ones included, stay and the portal adds the entries that are missing (*Always* also updates the values of the same entries). With version 1.1 of the plugin the portal reads which labels the site uses as filters and always writes those, first and beyond the specification limit. In the Warehouse the product sheet shows and saves the plugin rows when the product has them.
- The panel now lives at **/app**.
