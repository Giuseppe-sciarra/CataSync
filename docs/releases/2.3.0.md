A Guide tab with screenshots, so clients learn the portal from the portal itself.

### Highlights
- **Guide tab**: loading products in four steps, what each product status means and how to add, rename, order and delete categories, each with an example screenshot in Italian or English. The 📖 buttons on Products and Categories open the right section.
- **First-time invitation** to the Guide at the top of Products. Once dismissed it stays dismissed: the portal stores it on the user, so it holds on every browser and device.
- The old “How it works” panel moved from the Products page into the Guide.
- While a search is running, the row now says “Sheet on its way…” instead of “Sheet not found”.
