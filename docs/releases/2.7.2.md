A search log for the admin, searches in rounds, and correct product sheets from PrestaShop shops.

### Highlights
- **Search log** in *Settings*: every code searched by customers with its result and the reason (engines blocking, sites refusing, missing fields, source site), filters, the full «Details» diary and CSV export. No customer data: no customer, shop, user, upload name, product title or shop error messages.
- **Searches in rounds**: after 5 codes actually sent to the engines the portal pauses 2 minutes and resumes by itself (configurable in *Settings → Automatic web search*). Codes already in memory don't count; during the pause the upload bar shows when it resumes and publishing keeps going. Also applies to the batch test.
- Wrong sheets from PrestaShop shops fixed (e.g. 8801643654689 on chironpro.com): name and brand were taken from a related product (INTUIS) and the shop name («CHIRON Distribution») was used as the brand. Now only the product block carrying the code is read, the brand comes from the sheet's «Brand» label, and `large_default` photos are kept.
- Translation round-trip check: technical words LibreTranslate garbles («bibloc» → «Bilancio») stay as they were.
