The whole product sheet editable from the Warehouse, and a read-only Customer profile for clients.

### Highlights
- **Product sheet**: in the Warehouse, *Edit* (or a click on the name) opens the product as it is on the site — name, status, brand, SKU, EAN, price and sale price, stock, categories, photos, short description, description and specifications. *Save to the site* sends only what changed.
- **Photos**: reorder, remove, add by address or **upload from the computer** (JPG, PNG, WebP up to 8 MB; needs `PUBLIC_URL`, since the site downloads the file from the portal).
- **Descriptions** with a simple editor (bold, italics, subheadings, lists, links) or in HTML, cleaned before being written to the site. Descriptions holding videos, embeds or forms are left to WordPress; texts are read as stored (`context=edit`), so shortcodes are kept.
- **Customer profile** replaces the Shop tab: the client sees company details, logo, subscription (status, plan, expiry, monthly quota, whether publishing is allowed), connected sites and nightly update — read-only. The administrator edits everything, logo included, under *Clients*.
