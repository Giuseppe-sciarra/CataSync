Shop categories can now be managed from CataSync, and an optional WordPress mu-plugin keeps them in the site menu.

### Highlights
- **Categories tab**: create categories (also inside another one), delete them and put them in order by dragging them or with the arrows. The order is WooCommerce's own (*Products → Categories*).
- Deleting a category keeps its products on the site, moves its subcategories up one level and clears the portal's mappings to it.
- **New mu-plugin** `wordpress/catasync-category-menu.php` (also downloadable from the Categories tab): every category, created in CataSync or on the site, goes under the menu item you choose, subcategories under their category, in the same order. Renames follow, deleted categories disappear, items added by hand stay after the categories.
- The menu item is chosen in CataSync (*Categories → Site menu*) or on the site (*Appearance → Menu categorie*); the plugin uses the shop's WooCommerce REST key.
- Every fixed text of the interface is now checked for its translation in the CI.
